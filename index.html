A bakery born from love

Our Sweet Story

Soma's Cakes & Flakes is Kishanganj's beloved dessert atelier — a magical little bakery where pastry, playfulness, and premium craft meet.

Founded by baker Soma Saha, our kitchen is a candy-coloured world of whipped cream clouds, sprinkle rain, and cakes that feel like a celebration even on an ordinary Tuesday.

We bake with patience, premium ingredients, and a little extra joy — so every box that leaves the shop smells like vanilla, cocoa, and happiness.

  • Quality ingredients
  • Freshly baked daily
  • Made with passion
  • Designed to delight
  1. 2018 A dream in a home kitchen

    Soma started baking birthday cakes for neighbours in Kishanganj — each one more ambitious than the last.

  2. 2021 The first bakery counter

    Cakes & Flakes opened its doors, filling Station Road with the scent of butter cookies and warm bread.

  3. Today Kishanganj's celebration bakery

    From custom wedding cakes to midnight donut cravings, we craft desserts that become memories.

Founder & Head Baker

The heart of the bakery

Meet Soma Saha

Soma believes a cake is a love letter you can taste. Raised with festival sweets and family recipes, she turned a quiet passion into Kishanganj's most joyful bakery.

Every frosting swirl is hers. Every custom brief is listened to like a story. She still tastes the batter, still ties the ribbon, still greets guests as if they walked into her home.

“I bake so people feel celebrated — even if the celebration is simply that they made it through the week.”

Her commitment is simple: premium ingredients, honest flavours, and customer happiness that lingers longer than the last crumb.
